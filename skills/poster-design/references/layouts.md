# Layout recipes by poster type

All derived from the reference images in `refs/`. Pick one, don't invent a new
skeleton unless the brief demands it.

## 1. Property / listing ("House For Sale")
- Photo top 55–60%, full bleed, bottom edge cut by a diagonal or curve.
- Brand-color panel below: huge headline left, white info card right
  (price header + ✓ feature grid: beds/baths/sqft in two columns).
- Footer strip: phone + website on tiny icons, address.
- Logo + company name in a small white tab, top-left.

## 2. Service promo ("Lawn Mowing 30% Off", "Laundry Service")
- Solid brand-color background, rounded white inner frame optional.
- Left: headline + sub-badge ("Service Deals" ribbon) + ✓ benefit list.
- Right: photo in a rounded-rect or arch frame with thick white border.
- Rotated circular discount sticker ("30% Off") overlapping the photo corner.
- Pill CTA + contact row at bottom.

## 3. Product sale ("Headphones M3000 PRO", "Camera DSLR 3000")
- Split: colored upper block (headline + small body) / light lower block.
- Product photo centered on the split line, overlapping both.
- Price in a badge or bold accent text; "PROMO 20% OFF" circle sticker.
- Logo top-right, BUY NOW pill bottom-left, website bottom-right.

## 4. Two-tone bold announce ("NOW SELLING", "Heavy Equipment For Rent")
- Vertical split: photo half / flat color half.
- Color half: condensed uppercase stack, one line in accent color.
- One small kicker line above, one CTA line below. Nothing else.

## 5. Engagement post ("What's your hot take?", "Agree or Disagree?")
- Either: neon/flat color half + candid photo half, headline in giant
  condensed caps; or calm photo background with serif question overlaid.
- Tiny instruction line: "share it in the comments. we read every one."
- No contact furniture — just handle or logo.

## 6. Testimonial card
- Soft cream/muted background, serif display heading ("Customer Testimonial").
- White speech-bubble card: avatar circle, name, ★★★★★, 2–3 line quote.
- Phone mockup or product photo bleeding in from one edge.
- Sparkle/leaf line-art accents in corners only.

## 7. Elegant listing ("Elegant House For Sale", navy/photo collage)
- Deep solid background (navy, forest), photos in angled/diamond crops
  stacked on the right.
- Left: serif or script accent line inside a bold sans stack,
  "FEATURES" label + ✓ list, contact block bottom.

## Canvas sizes
- 1080×1080 IG/FB square (default)
- 1080×1350 IG portrait
- 1080×1920 story
- 1200×630 FB/link banner
