# Reference images

The posters in `refs/` are the ground truth for the look — Read them before
designing. Each one demonstrates one layout recipe from `layouts.md`. They are
original recreations of proven small-business template patterns (not copied
templates), rendered at 1080×1080.

- `house-for-sale-green.png` — recipe 1: photo top ~58% with angled cut, green
  panel below, white price card with ✓ feature grid, contact footer, logo tab.
- `lawn-mowing.png` — recipe 2: flat green service promo, arch photo frame with
  thick white border, rotated 30% sticker, yellow ribbon + pill CTA.
- `headphones-promo.png` — recipe 3: navy upper block / light lower, product
  card overlapping the curved split, promo circle, price, BUY NOW pill.
- `now-selling-blue.png` — recipe 4: vertical photo/flat-blue split, giant
  condensed uppercase stack, small kicker above, CTA line below.
- `hot-take-neon.png` — recipe 5: neon yellow half + candid portrait half,
  one-word-per-line Anton stack, tiny comment instruction at the bottom.
- `testimonial-plant.png` — recipe 6: cream ground, serif italic heading,
  white speech-bubble card with avatar + stars + quote, tilted phone frame.
- `elegant-house-navy.png` — recipe 7: deep navy, bold sans + script accent
  line, FEATURES label + ✓ list, diamond photo crops stacked right.
