# Reference images

The posters in `refs/` are the ground truth for the look — **Read them before
designing**, pick the one closest to the brief, and match its level of polish.
They are real template-grade designs at 1080×1080.

## Property / real estate
- `real-estate-grey-diagonal.png` — muted grey diagonal panels over a building
  photo, pennant-shaped logo tab top-right, "Contact Us" list with circle icons,
  dotted-grid decoration, white-outline BOOK NOW.
- `realty-search-bar.png` — minimal: full-bleed dusk photo with a floating
  search-bar graphic ("3BR/2TB home for sale near me"), calm light footer with
  just brand name + one line. Restraint as a style.
- `elegant-house-blue-diamonds.png` — blue monochrome, bold caps + script
  "For Sale", diamond photo collage falling down the right, FEATURES arrow
  banner, bullet list.
- `modern-home-red-frame.png` — red border framing the whole canvas,
  translucent red headline panel over the hero photo, two red-framed inset
  photos right, feature bullets in two columns, icon contact rows.
- `modern-home-brown-arrow.png` — sky photo background, dark-brown arrow-shaped
  headline banner, serif-free bullets, letterspaced CONTACT US pill, icon rows.
- `modern-home-price-strip.png` — photo top half, white band with chevron
  decorations + three-line centered headline (orange/dark/orange), dark footer
  strip: bullets | "Price Starting From $ + 25% OFF + CTA" | paragraph, orange
  contact bar at the very bottom.

## Hotel / resort
- `luxury-resort-green-script.png` — dark green with subtle pattern, huge
  orange script "luxury" over white serif RESORT, circle photo crops top-right
  and bottom-left, orange circle feature icons, white rounded Features and
  Booking bars.
- `luxury-hotel-olive-collage.png` — white left column (script accent + olive
  caps stack, paragraph, pill CTA, icon contacts), vertical stack of rounded
  photo cards center, full-bleed resort photo right.
- `modern-hotel-black-circles.png` — black diagonal blocks on white, overlapping
  white-ringed circle photo crops, right-aligned headline, justified body text,
  angled photo panels top-left and bottom-right.

## Retail / promo
- `furniture-sale-olive.png` — one real room scene as the whole background,
  olive-green text block on the right (caps headline, paragraph, huge 40% OFF,
  pill SHOP NOW), no panels — typography sits on the photo's clean wall.
- `fashion-sale-yellow-split.png` — vertical yellow/grey split, people cutout
  straddling the seam, right-aligned condensed caps headline, zigzag squiggle
  decoration, 40% OFF + yellow pill.

## Festival / seasonal
- `lunar-new-year-red.png` — flat illustration style: red ground, gold
  line-art clouds/lanterns/columns, arched headline text, central circular
  motif, small info frames left and right. No photo at all.
- `new-year-sale-red-gold.png` — deep red radial ground, gold fireworks line
  art, everything centered and stacked: number, giant caps, cream badge bar,
  50% OFF, pill CTA. Symmetry done on purpose.

Patterns these add beyond the layout recipes: pennant/arrow-shaped banners,
translucent color panels over photos, diamond and circle photo collages,
full-canvas border frames, search-bar graphic as hero element, flat
illustration festival style, and centered symmetric sale layouts (allowed for
festival/sale posts).
